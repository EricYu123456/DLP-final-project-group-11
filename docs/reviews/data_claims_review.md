# Data and provenance claims review

Reviewer: independent research/data agent. Reviewed 2026-10-28, Asia/Taipei. This review covers the frozen data artifacts and source wording. It does not assess predictive performance or rerun the 4.6-million-row scan.

## Claims supported by the saved audit

- The source is pinned to `RZ412/EmbedLLM` revision `266ae3e180ccb533c86afc9cfb2f5db99d171aea`; [`dataset_source.json`](../../data/metadata/dataset_source.json) gives sizes and SHA-256 hashes for all nine downloaded files. [`summary.json`](../../reports/data_audit/summary.json) records a complete scan of 4,586,288 CSV rows, 112 models, 35,673 prompt IDs, and 3,995,376 model/prompt cells. It reports zero cells without a CSV row, 553,616 cells with duplicate rows, and 77,581 cells with conflicting binary labels. These counts are consistent with a full 112 × 35,673 grid, but row existence alone cannot establish that upstream inference succeeded.
- Every published binary tensor cell equals the maximum label in its corresponding raw CSV rows in this revision, with zero mismatches. This is an empirical property of the released files, **not** evidence that `max` was the authors' intended scoring rule. The released CSV lacks raw responses, evaluator revision, filter identity, generation settings, and failure status. The [EmbedLLM paper, Section 4.2](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/bf5e4b85d203481d6e37bd32d9600162-Paper-Conference.pdf) describes 36,054 questions and binary labels from `lm-evaluation-harness`; the pinned release has 35,673 prompt IDs. Treat the paper and release as different observable versions.
- The five-source primary cohort follows a value-independent structural rule: MMLU, MedMCQA, MathQA, TruthfulQA, and LogiQA prompts must have one raw row per audited model and one category per prompt. All 112 released models have the same singleton eligibility pattern for these sources. Of 22,651 candidate prompt IDs, 22,624 pass the structural rule. One representative per candidate underlying-question group removes 149 more, leaving 22,475 questions and 88 included models. The saved [`primary_selection.json`](../../reports/data_audit/primary_selection.json) agrees with the audit fingerprint and contains 88 model IDs and 22,475 prompt IDs. The retained counts by source are MMLU 13,841; MedMCQA 4,182; MathQA 2,985; TruthfulQA 817; LogiQA 650.
- The saved [`split_strict_random.json`](../../reports/data_audit/split_strict_random.json) has 62/13/13 train/validation/test models and 15,733/3,372/3,370 train/validation/test representative questions. These add to the frozen cohort. Split claims still require the experiment runner and final lock to use this same fingerprint and manifest.
- [`model_families.csv`](../../data/metadata/model_families.csv) contains exactly 112 assignments: 88 included and 24 excluded. Included family counts match the [data source narrative](../research/data_sources.md): Llama 36, Mistral 18, Yi 9, Qwen 6, DeepSeek 5, Pythia 3, Gemma/MPT/Phi 2 each, and five singletons. All 95 distinct URLs referenced by included models appear as either a source or resolved URL in the 99-row [`family_evidence.csv`](../../data/metadata/family_evidence.csv); every evidence row has an HTTP 200 snapshot and 64-character SHA-256. This establishes traceability of the classification evidence, not the exact weight revision evaluated by EmbedLLM.

## Boundaries for the final report

1. Call the target a **released binary-label proxy on a five-benchmark representative subset**. A per-benchmark mean here is not an official full-benchmark score. MedMCQA and MMLU source-item mappings are documented in [`data_sources.md`](../research/data_sources.md); their observed source splits should not be generalized to other benchmarks.
2. Call family groups conservative ancestry/series blocks. Llama generations and distinct DeepSeek series are grouped for leakage control without a claim of shared checkpoints. Some evidence-card revisions postdate the dataset release; card provenance cannot establish the historical evaluated checkpoint. Family holdout reduces known within-block overlap but cannot prove independence of training corpora or unreviewed cross-family ancestry.
3. The grouping rule uses released prompt text and can overgroup identical generic stems or miss paraphrases. The 22,475-question population is a deterministic selected subset. The one-record requirement is retrospective: a truly new model must be collected and scored under a compatible single-record protocol before its scores can be compared prospectively.
4. No final-test accuracy, improvement, uncertainty, or deployment claim follows from the audit. The final report should include such statements only after checking the configuration lock, validation-only selection, saved prediction grid, common probe IDs, and final test artifacts.

The [official `mf.py` router preprocessing](https://github.com/richardzhuang0412/EmbedLLM/blob/9b276305af1342188c2fc066e41d5dc8bdfea895/algorithm/mf.py#L192-L213) has separate overwrite/zero-fill behavior; it should not be used to infer how the published y tensors were generated.
