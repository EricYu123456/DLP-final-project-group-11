The metric implementation gives benchmarks equal weight within each model, then models equal weight. Per-item proper scores use the same observed response mask as score errors. ## Final result review

The final main and original family outputs pass the checks in [the final evaluation audit](../../reports/reviews/final_evaluation_audit.json). Main results retain the pretest lock and contain all 5,304 model-metric rows, 26,520 benchmark rows, and 2,457 converged neural/diagnostic MAP fits. The test set has thirteen models and 3,370 retained questions. Nine models are in the conservative Llama block; Gemma, Mistral, Phi, and Yi contribute one each. This is five lineage blocks, not thirteen independent families or thirty-nine independent seed-model observations.

Under balanced random probes, low-rank macro MAE is 6.258/5.099/4.274 percentage points at 50/100/200 labels; scalar calibration gives 6.195/5.611/4.780. The approximately half-point low-rank advantage at 100/200 is a point-estimate improvement whose paired five-block intervals include zero. The scalar baseline is slightly better at 50. Claims of uniform dominance or a decisive population-wide improvement are unsupported.

Global Fisher improves over global random at 100 and 200 in this run, while balanced random has the lowest point error at 200. Benchmark balancing and semantic diversity are not uniformly helpful. The response-alignment diagnostic is more specific: within-benchmark permutation worsens low-rank MAE by 0.188/0.760/0.912 percentage points, while rank zero is exactly invariant. This supports useful item-specific response information for low rank. The structured variant's permutation results do not support the same interpretation for that variant.

Original family outputs contain 1,632 model-metric rows, 8,160 benchmark rows, and 612 converged MAP fits. All persisted static probe IDs match both arms and the pretest plan. Related-model inclusion usually helps the tested Llama and Yi cases; the Mistral effect is small and reverses at the larger budget. These are conditional training-composition contrasts for three broad series blocks. They do not isolate a causal ancestry effect or prove performance on arbitrary unseen families.

The original family study evaluates structured rank 8 and rank 0 only. A separate low-rank family supplement was subsequently proposed after the main final table had been inspected. Its variant choice also follows main validation involving family-target models. That analysis must remain explicitly exploratory, with its own timestamps, original numerical training defaults, fresh weights, Qwen-only prior selection, and separate provenance. It does not retroactively change the original confirmatory instrument or this acceptance record.
